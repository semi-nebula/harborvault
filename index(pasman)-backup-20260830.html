One account, a private vault for everyone in the family.

Prototype note: accounts are stored only in this browser (passwords are hashed). True sign-in from any device needs an online backend, which comes later.

Manage collections
Collections belong to this vault only.

Deleting a collection moves its passwords into the first remaining collection.

What is guest mode?
Use the vault without an account.

Guest mode works instantly and still gives you profiles with their own master keys - but everything is saved only on this device, in this browser: the data cannot be synced between devices and will not appear if you sign in somewhere else. Create an account to keep the same profiles after switching devices (once online sync is added).
